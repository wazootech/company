# Agent guidelines

## What this repo is

The Wazoo company handbook: the team roster, Discord agents, working norms, and
the contributing path. Slow-changing content by design.

Tooling, environments and deploy detail do not belong here. Working rules live
in `wazootech/workspace` and decisions in `wazootech/memory`; link to them
instead of restating them. See
[repository lanes](https://github.com/wazootech/workspace/blob/main/AGENTS.md#repository-lanes).

## How to work here

- Read [README.md](README.md) and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before
  working anywhere in the org.
- Treat docs here as durable company context. Keep claims precise and avoid
  inventing policy, strategy, or commitments.
- Preserve existing document structure and naming conventions.
- Use sentence-case markdown headings and avoid decorative headings.
- Before finishing, check links and summarize the documents changed.
